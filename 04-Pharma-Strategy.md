# Pharma Manufacturing Strategy: SA / Eswatini / Rwanda

## Vision
Close African pharmaceutical import deficit by building local manufacturing partnerships in 3 countries. Target: $50M+ import substitution by 2028.

## Country Strategy

### South Africa (PRIMARY HUB)
- **Why:** Mature pharma sector, existing GMP infrastructure, SADC gateway
- **Market:** ~$3.5B annual pharma market, ~70% imported
- **Opportunity:** $500M+ local manufacturing gap
- **Play:** Partner with existing manufacturers (idle capacity utilization)
- **Timeline:** Q4 2026 partnership signed, Q2 2027 production

**Target Contacts:**
- ASPEN Pharmacare (idle capacity?)
- Cipla SA
- Pharmacare Development Company
- PDSA (Pharmaceutical Distributors SA)
- SAMI (SA Medicines Initiative)

### Eswatini (LOGISTICS HUB)
- **Why:** SADC member, lower corporate tax, strategic logistics position
- **Market:** ~$150M pharma market, 90%+ imported
- **Opportunity:** Regional distribution hub (SA/Botswana/Lesotho/Namibia)
- **Play:** Establish regional warehouse + packaging facility
- **Timeline:** Q2 2027 operation launch

**Target Contacts:**
- Swazi Trading Company
- ESWATINI Standard Board
- Regional SADC trade bodies

### Rwanda (GROWTH HUB)
- **Why:** Tech-forward government, AfCFTA leader, Special Economic Zones
- **Market:** ~$200M pharma market (growing 15% YoY), 85% imported
- **Opportunity:** Generic manufacturing + distribution (East Africa)
- **Play:** Build manufacturing SEZ facility (government support likely)
- **Timeline:** Q1 2027 regulatory approval, Q4 2027 production

**Target Contacts:**
- Rwanda Development Board (RDB)
- East African Pharmaceutical Manufacturers Association
- UNIDO (industrial partnerships)
- AfCFTA secretariat

## Product Focus
1. **Generic pharmaceuticals** (antibiotics, anti-malaria, pain relief)
2. **Chronic disease** (diabetes, hypertension treatments)
3. **Pediatric formulations** (high-margin, high-impact)

## Success Metrics
- **Year 1:** 1 manufacturing partnership signed (SA or Rwanda)
- **Year 2:** $20M import substitution (combined 3 countries)
- **Year 3:** $50M+ annual revenue, 3-country presence operational

## Risk Mitigation
- Regulatory delays: Start with packaging/repacking (lower barriers)
- Supply chain: Partner with existing regional distributors
- Market: Focus on essential medicines (recession-proof)
- Currency: Price in ZAR, USD, RWF (hedging strategy TBD by Katlego)
