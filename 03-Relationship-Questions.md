# Relationship Foundation: Daily Questions & Knowledge Base

**Purpose:** Build intimacy through intention. Agents ask, you both answer, Obsidian captures the pattern of your future together.

## Daily Question Rotation (Bunny Bot)

### Monday: Gratitude & Appreciation
- "What did the other person do this week that made you feel supported?"
- "How did we show up for each other financially/operationally?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Gratitude/`

### Tuesday: Pharma & Business Partnership
- "What excited you most about our manufacturing strategy this week?"
- "How can we align better on financial decisions?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Business/`

### Wednesday: Travel & Adventure
- "Where do you most want to travel together next?"
- "What would a perfect week in China/Thailand look like for us?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Travel-Dreams/`

### Thursday: Health & Wellness
- "How are we prioritizing our health while building the business?"
- "What does 'health leadership' mean to us as partners?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Health/`

### Friday: Future Visioning
- "What do we want to be known for in 5 years?"
- "How are agents helping us live the life we want?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Vision/`

### Saturday: Intimacy & Connection
- "What made you feel most connected to me this week?"
- "How can we make more time for each other?"
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Connection/`

### Sunday: Weekly Reflection
- Joint reflection on the week
- Financial review with Katlego + Tumelo
- Planning next week's priorities
- Log to: `Bunny-Rabbit/Relationship/Weekly-Sync/`

## Knowledge Base Building
Every answer feeds into Obsidian vault. Agents learn:
- Your values
- Decision-making patterns
- Risk tolerance (especially financial)
- Travel preferences
- Health priorities
- Communication style

**Bunny Bot reads this knowledge base to personalize:**
- Pharma deal recommendations (aligned with values)
- Travel routing (aligned with preferences)
- Investment decisions (aligned with risk tolerance)
