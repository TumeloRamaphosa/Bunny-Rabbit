## The ask
Raise ~**$5–8M CAPEX** across South Africa, Eswatini, and Rwanda to build local pharmaceutical manufacturing and close African import gaps. Target **$50M+ import substitution by 2028**.

## Why now
Africa still imports most essential medicines.
- **South Africa:** ~$3.5B annual pharma market, ~70% imported — primary GMP hub
- **Eswatini:** logistics / SADC packaging & distribution hub
- **Rwanda:** SEZ + AfCFTA growth hub for East Africa generics

## The play
1. **SA (primary)** — partner idle GMP capacity → partnership Q4 2026 → production Q2 2027
2. **Eswatini** — regional warehouse + packaging → ops Q2 2027
3. **Rwanda** — SEZ generics + pediatric → regulatory Q1 2027 → production Q4 2027

## Products
1. Generics (antibiotics, anti-malaria, pain relief)
2. Chronic disease (diabetes, hypertension)
3. Pediatric formulations (high-margin, high-impact)
